# Plays

A play links one kind of public event to one offer from the salesperson's company. Run only the
plays enabled in the Config tab. Search terms are starting points; vary them
and add the territory (for example "Deutschland", "Österreich", "Schweiz", or
a federal state).

Useful trade sources for all plays: channelpartner.de, it-business.de,
datacenter-insider.de, cio.de, computerwoche.de, it-daily.net,
datacenterdynamics.com, company press pages and investor news.

---

## Play 1: Migration → disposal

**Idea:** a company moves systems to the cloud, to a hosting provider or to a
new data centre. The hardware it leaves behind needs certified data erasure,
then resale or recycling.

**Type:** Seller.

**Signals**
- Move to SAP cloud (RISE with SAP, SAP Cloud ERP), hyperscaler migration,
  "lift and shift".
- IT operations or SAP hosting handed to a provider.
- Data centre relocation, consolidation or shutdown.
- Merger or carve-out where two IT estates are combined or split.
- Office or site closure with IT equipment.

**Search terms**
- `"RISE with SAP" Kunde Migration <month year>`
- `Rechenzentrum Umzug OR Konsolidierung OR Stilllegung <year>`
- `"verlagert" IT-Betrieb OR SAP-Betrieb Cloud Unternehmen`
- `data center migration Germany announces <year>`
- `Outsourcing IT-Infrastruktur Vertrag <year>`

**Contact titles:** Head of IT Infrastructure, Leiter IT-Infrastruktur, Data
Center Manager, IT Asset Manager, CIO at companies under about 2,000 staff.

**Caution:** the source rarely says what happens to the old hardware. Say so
in Why it fits, and ask in the note instead of assuming.

## Play 2: MSPs → partnership

**Idea:** systems houses and managed service providers resell hardware and
services to many end customers. A growing one needs a partner for refurbished
hardware, spare parts, and taking back customers' old equipment.

**Type:** Partner.

**Signals**
- A systems house or MSP buys another, merges, or opens a new location.
- New managed-infrastructure or data centre service launched.
- New investor or buy-and-build strategy.
- Named in rankings of growing systems houses.

**Search terms**
- `Systemhaus Übernahme <month year>`
- `MSP übernimmt OR Fusion Systemhaus <year>`
- `site:channelpartner.de Systemhaus <year>`
- `site:it-business.de Übernahme Systemhaus`
- `Managed Service Provider neuer Standort <year>`

**Contact titles:** Geschäftsführer or Managing Director at firms under about
100 staff; otherwise Head of Purchasing, Leiter Einkauf, Head of Partner
Management, Head of Infrastructure Sales.

## Play 3: GPU build-outs

**Idea:** AI clouds and research sites buy GPU servers and later replace them
with newer generations. They need sourcing now and secure buy-back later.

**Type:** Buyer, or Both when an upgrade replaces older hardware.

**Signals**
- Funding round or debt financing for GPU capacity.
- New AI data centre or cluster announced, or an existing one expanded.
- Move to a newer GPU generation.
- University or research HPC procurement or replacement.

**Search terms**
- `GPU Cluster Ausbau OR Erweiterung <year>`
- `KI-Rechenzentrum Finanzierung <year>`
- `AI cloud Germany raises GPU <year>`
- `Hochleistungsrechner Ablösung OR Nachfolger Universität <year>`

**Contact titles:** Head of Procurement or Supply Chain, Head of Data Center
Operations, Head of Infrastructure, CTO at firms under about 200 staff.

## Play 4: Public tenders

**Idea:** public bodies must tender for IT disposal, data erasure and hardware
purchases. A tender is a dated, confirmed need.

**Type:** Seller for disposal and erasure tenders; Buyer for hardware tenders
that allow refurbished equipment.

**Sources**
- TED: ted.europa.eu
- Germany: oeffentlichevergabe.de, dtvp.de, evergabe-online.de
- Austria: ausschreibungen.usp.gv.at
- Switzerland: simap.ch

**Search terms**
- `Ausschreibung Datenlöschung OR Datenträgervernichtung <year>`
- `Ausschreibung Verwertung OR Aussonderung IT-Altgeräte`
- `Ausschreibung Rücknahme Hardware Remarketing`
- `Ausschreibung Server gebraucht OR refurbished`
- `site:ted.europa.eu Datenlöschung IT-Hardware`

**Reading tenders:** some portals block automated reading (TED does). A
tender counts only when its notice, or a portal page showing the authority,
subject and deadline, was actually opened. A search-result title alone is not
a source. When the portals cannot be read, say so in the Run log and suggest
that the salesperson set up the portals' own email alerts.

**Contact:** the contracting authority's procurement office (Vergabestelle).
The LinkedIn note cell reads `Tender: respond through the portal by <deadline>.`
There is no cold outreach to public bodies. Put the deadline in Event date.
Drop tenders whose deadline has passed.

## Priority rules

**Priority 1** needs all of:
- a source published in the last 30 days, or an event date still ahead;
- a clear link from the event to a service in Config's services to offer;
- a company the salesperson can realistically reach (roughly 50 to 5,000
  staff, or any systems house or MSP);
- a contact title that owns the problem.

**Priority 2:** a real signal, with one weakness. Typical: a very large
company, a source 30 to 60 days old, or a hardware link that is likely but
unstated.

**Priority 3:** worth watching, not worth contacting yet. Typical: public
bodies without an open tender, or early announcements with no dates.

**Public bodies in any play** (universities, research centres, authorities,
public IT providers): always Priority 3 unless an open tender exists. Write no
outreach note. The LinkedIn note cell reads `No cold outreach: public
institution. Watch for a tender on <subject>.` and the Sales Navigator cell
reads `Not needed: respond through the tender portal if a tender appears.`

When the salesperson has marked the same pattern Not a fit three times, treat
that pattern as Priority 3.
