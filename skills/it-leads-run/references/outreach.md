# Outreach drafts

The salesperson sends every message by hand from their own LinkedIn or Sales
Navigator account. The draft has to be something they would be comfortable
sending without editing.

## LinkedIn note

- **Length:** 300 characters or fewer, counted with code. This is LinkedIn's
  limit for a connection note.
- **Language:** Config's note language. Default is German with "Sie" for
  companies in Germany, Austria and German-speaking Switzerland. Use English
  when Config says so or when the source shows the company works in English.
- **Shape:** three parts.
  1. The real event, named specifically.
  2. One service from Config's services to offer that fits it.
  3. One soft question.
- **Greeting:** `Guten Tag Herr/Frau <Nachname>,` when a person is named in
  the source; otherwise `Guten Tag,`. In English: `Hello <first name>,` or
  `Hello,`.
- **Services:** only those in Config's services to offer.

**Never**
- State anything about the prospect that the source does not say.
- Claim results, customer names, prices or certifications for the salesperson's company.
- Assume the hardware outcome. Ask: "Falls dabei Hardware frei wird ...".
- Use hype words (revolutionär, führend, einzigartig, game-changing), emoji,
  exclamation marks, or dashes used as punctuation.
- Mention that the lead came from an AI or an automated search.

### Examples

Seller, German:

> Guten Tag Herr Beispiel, ich habe gelesen, dass Sie Ihre SAP-Systeme in die Cloud verlagern. Falls dabei Server frei werden: Wir übernehmen zertifizierte Datenlöschung, Ankauf und Recycling. Wäre ein kurzer Austausch dazu für Sie interessant?

Partner, German:

> Guten Tag Frau Muster, Glückwunsch zur Übernahme. Wir arbeiten als Partner für Systemhäuser: geprüfte Enterprise-Hardware, Ersatzteile und die Rücknahme von Altgeräten Ihrer Kunden. Wäre das für Ihr Team ein Thema?

Buyer, English:

> Hello, I read about the financing for your new AI data centre. We source GPUs and enterprise hardware and later buys back older generations securely. Would it help to compare notes ahead of your next build?

(All three are under 300 characters. Always count the real draft with code.)

## Sales Navigator search recipe

Write one line the salesperson can follow in under a minute:

`Account: <company name> · Titles: <title 1> OR <title 2> OR <title 3> · Geography: <country> · Seniority: <levels>`

- Give German and English title variants (for example
  `"Leiter IT-Infrastruktur" OR "Head of IT Infrastructure"`).
- For firms under about 100 staff, add `Geschäftsführer OR "Managing Director"`.
- For Play 4 (tenders) write `Not needed: respond through the tender portal.`
